When each English region is written into the draft

Nine regions. Two kinds of date. The first is in the draft regulations. The second is the NRLA’s deadline, by midnight.

LandlordZONE (Helen Gregory, shown on the page as 9 September 2026, 11:51 AM) says the service starts in the West Midlands from 15 December, then the other regions on the same start dates, and that landlords have three months to register before councils can start enforcement. It does not print the “by midnight” dates. The NRLA does. The NRLA says West Midlands landlords must register by 14 March 2027, and sets out the rest of the midnight column below.

The NRLA also says the service opens on 15 December 2026 for landlords in England, and that a property may be registered from that day even if the region’s rules are not in force yet. LandlordZONE says landlords can register before they are required to. The draft instrument does not use those sentences. It says regulation 1 and regulation 3 come into force on 15 December 2026, and that the remaining regulations come into force for entries in each region on the dates in the middle column. For all other purposes, those remaining regulations come into force on 15 September 2027.

LandlordZONE says the date follows the property, not the landlord’s home. A landlord living in London with a rental property in Birmingham is, on that account, on the West Midlands date. This table does not look up a postcode.

The fee the articles state

Both the NRLA and LandlordZONE say £65 a year for each property. That payment, if it is the one charged, is made on the government service, not on this page. The draft regulations do not state £65. They say the database operator determines the fee.

Who the draft regulations say may make the entry

Your agent cannot press submit for you. Regulation 4 allows the entry to be made by the residential landlord, or by a person in one of the capacities in Schedule 2 paragraph 17 (attorney, personal representative, and the other roles listed there). A letting agent is not in that list. Regulation 4(5) does allow a letting agent or property manager to provide the Part 2 information. The NRLA and LandlordZONE both say the landlord still has to start registration.

Areas named in Schedule 1

The draft regulations say a named region has the extent of the areas of the councils listed. Those areas are the areas of those authorities as they existed on 15 December 2026. Naming a county council describes the area, as the instrument does. It is not a statement about which council would enforce anything.
